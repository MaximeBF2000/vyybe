# Playbook — ad creatives (Meta, LinkedIn, display)

Ads are interruptions: stop the scroll, make one promise, show proof, ask for one action.

## Structure
- **Visual hook** (top 60%): product in use, the outcome, a bold before/after, a striking number, a person reacting. Real product UI (`capture_webpage`) or product imagery beats abstract shapes.
- **Headline** ≤ 7 words: benefit or pain ("Stop losing leads at 2am").
- **Proof** (optional, only real): rating, logo strip of real customers, a number the user gave you.
- **CTA button** ≤ 3 words ("Start free", "Get the guide"), high-contrast pill.
- **Logo** small, consistent corner.

## Angles to produce when asked for variants
Pain → solution · Outcome/result · Social proof · Feature spotlight · Comparison (us vs old way) · Offer/urgency (only with a real offer). Each variant = a different angle, same brand system.

## Multi-size sets
1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1080×1920 (keep text out of top 250 / bottom 340), 1200×628. **Recompose** for each ratio (move/resize layers), keep identical copy and hierarchy. Put them in one `collection`.

## Rules
- Text ≤ ~20% of the area when there's a strong visual.
- Never invent claims, prices, discounts, testimonials or customer logos.
- Brand fonts/colors mandatory when a branding exists.
