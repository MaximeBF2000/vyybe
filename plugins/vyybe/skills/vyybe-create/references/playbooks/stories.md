# Playbook — stories, reels covers, TikTok covers (1080×1920)

Seen full-screen on a phone, often for 2–5 seconds, with platform UI on top.

- **Safe zones**: keep text and key subjects out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px (Instagram); TikTok also covers the right 140 px and bottom 480 px.
- Center of gravity in the middle third. One message, ≤ 20 words, very large type (90–140 px headlines).
- Full-bleed imagery works best (photo, generated scene, product); add a scrim for legibility.
- Story versions of a post: recompose vertically (stack hook → visual → CTA), don't letterbox the square design.
- Reel/TikTok covers: like a thumbnail — 2–4 words + a striking subject, consistent series style.
- Interactive-feeling elements (sticker-like "poll" bubbles, "link in bio" pills) are fine as visuals.
