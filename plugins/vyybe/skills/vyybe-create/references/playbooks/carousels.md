# Playbook — Instagram & LinkedIn carousels (1080×1350)

Carousels win on **saves and shares**: people swipe because slide 1 promises value and each slide pays off. They're seen at phone width (~390 px); the cover also appears cropped in the profile grid.

## Slide 1 — the cover sells the swipe
- A hook in ≤ 10 words (see content.md patterns) at 100–150 px, readable in the feed at 390 px.
- A **visual idea**, not just text: a real UI mock, an object, a bold number, a before/after, a face, a sticker. The cover should look like the series' "poster".
- Swipe cue (→, "swipe", a shape crossing the right edge). Optional page counter / series label.
- Profile grid crops to ~3:4 — keep the hook inside the central area.

## Middle slides — one payoff each
- Each slide = one idea: headline (60–100 px) + 1–3 lines + a **proof element** (example, mini UI, command, number, diagram, quote, before/after). Text-only middle slides are the #1 cause of boring carousels.
- Keep a strict system: same margins, kicker/number position, type scale, colors. Vary the composition and the proof element.
- Continuity devices: a line or shape that continues across slide edges, progress dots/numbers, consistent illustration style.
- Real assets: `find_logo` for every tool mentioned, `capture_webpage` for real screens, framed in browser/phone mocks.

## Last slide — recap + one CTA
- Summary (3 bullets or a checklist) + one CTA ("Save this", "Follow @handle for…", "Comment 'GUIDE'"), handle/logo.
- LinkedIn: a short "who I am" line helps follows.

## LinkedIn specifics
- Exported as PDF; slightly more text is fine (≤ 40 words/slide), professional but human. Frameworks, checklists, data, lessons learned perform well.
- Strong covers: "X lessons from…", "The framework I use for…", numbers, a clean diagram.

## Instagram specifics
- Punchier, more visual, 5–8 slides. Bold color systems and recurring characters/objects make a series recognizable.

## Self-check (use `preview {mode: "context"}` once)
- Cover in the phone feed: does it stop the scroll? Is the hook legible at 390 px?
- Each slide: one idea, a proof element, nothing below ~26 px.
- Does slide 2 deliver on slide 1 immediately (no throat-clearing)?
