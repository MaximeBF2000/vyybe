# Playbook — single posts, announcements, quotes, promos

Single images compete in a fast feed. One message, one focal point, readable at phone width.

## Types and what makes them work
- **Launch / announcement** ("we shipped dark mode", "new pistachio croissant"): the product is the hero — real screenshot in a device mock, a product photo/generated product shot with dramatic light, or the feature visualized. Big claim + date/CTA. Celebration devices: badges ("NEW", "LIVE"), confetti-like shapes in brand colors, a sticker.
- **Quote / insight**: the quote is the hero — set like an editorial pull quote (large serif or heavy sans, quote marks as graphic element), author photo/name small. One emphasized phrase.
- **Tip / stat**: a huge number or a single tip with a tiny visual proof (mini chart drawn in divs, before/after).
- **Event / webinar**: speaker photo(s), title, date/time, CTA, logo. Clear hierarchy: title > date > speaker.
- **Hiring / milestone / testimonial**: real people and real numbers only (never invent).
- **Local business** (bakery, gym, café…): appetizing/real imagery first (user photos or generated/stock), warm palette, handwritten or editorial accents, price/date/place clearly.

## Rules
- Research the subject (product UI, brand colors, the event) before designing.
- Use the real brand logo(s) via `find_logo` when brands are mentioned.
- Layers: background mood → hero → text → small badge/CTA.
- Square or 4:5; keep the key content in the center 80% (feeds crop).
- Check with `preview {mode: "context"}`.
